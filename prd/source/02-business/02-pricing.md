## 2. Pricing Strategy

### 2.1 Strategy Type

**Value-based pricing** with freemium model. Free tier establishes market presence; paid tiers capture value from teams and enterprises who derive measurable ROI from the component library.

### 2.2 Pricing Rationale

| Factor | Decision | Rationale |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| **Freemium** | Core library free | Maximizes adoption, community contributions |
| **Per-seat (Pro)** | $19/mo per developer | Aligns cost with team size |
| **Flat-rate (Enterprise)** | $500+/mo | Predictable for enterprise budgets |
| **Annual discount** | 17% off ($199 vs $228) | Encourage commitment, reduce churn |
| **No per-project** | Team-based licensing | Simplifies billing, reduces friction |

|------|-----------|-----------|--------|----------|

| **Pro pricing** | $19/mo | $29/mo | Revenue per user | 60 days |
| **Free component count** | 50 free | 70 free | Pro conversion | 90 days |
| **Trial length** | 14 days | 30 days | Activation rate | 60 days |

### 6.2 Pricing Signals to Watch

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| High free → Pro conversion (>15%) | Price may be too low |
| Low free → Pro conversion (<5%) | Price may be too high or value unclear |
| High churn in month 1–2 | Onboarding or value delivery issue |
| Enterprise inquiries without conversion | Pricing or feature gap |

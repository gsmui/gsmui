| **Annual churn (Pro)** | < 40% | SaaS average: 40–60% |
| **Annual churn (Enterprise)** | < 20% | SaaS average: 15–25% |

### 4.2 Churn Drivers

| Driver | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|--------|------------|--------|------------|
| **Price sensitivity** | High | High | Annual discounts, value communication |
| **Feature gaps** | Medium | High | Roadmap transparency, feature velocity |
| **Switching to competitor** | Medium | Medium | Community lock-in, ecosystem depth |
| **Project cancellation** | Low | Low | Not addressable — natural churn |
| **Poor support experience** | Medium | High | SLA enforcement, support training |

### 4.3 Retention Levers
